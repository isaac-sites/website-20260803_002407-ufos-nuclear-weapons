## Introduction

The green fireball sightings of 1948–1949 became the most important early [UFO investigation]({{ 'ufo-case-investigation/' | relative_url }}) connected with Los Alamos because they combined unusual aerial observations with the security concerns of the nuclear age. Bright green objects were repeatedly reported over New Mexico, including near Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory, Sandia facilities, and other military locations. The events did not produce evidence of [extraterrestrial]({{ 'extraterrestrial/' | relative_url }}) craft, but they did lead scientists and military officials to conduct one of the earliest serious investigations of unexplained aerial phenomena in the United States.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://warufo.com/document/295" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: warufo.com">[WARUFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">warufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DOE-UAP-D004, Los Alamos Conference on Aerial Phenomena, 1949 — WARUFO — Declassified UAP Document ArchiveJuly 10, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 10, 2026</span></span></span>

What made the case distinctive was not simply that people saw strange lights. The reports came during a period when officials were highly alert to possible [foreign technology]({{ 'foreign-risks/' | relative_url }}), missile development, and threats to nuclear facilities. The investigations brought together meteor experts, physicists, intelligence personnel, and military organisations, creating a historical link between UFO studies and national security concerns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://warufo.com/document/295" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: warufo.com">[WARUFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">warufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DOE-UAP-D004, Los Alamos Conference on Aerial Phenomena, 1949 — WARUFO — Declassified UAP Document ArchiveJuly 10, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 10, 2026</span></span></span>

## The 1948 sightings and witness reports

The green fireball wave began in late 1948, when observers across New Mexico reported brilliant green lights moving through the sky. Witnesses often described them as much brighter and more unusual than ordinary meteors: some appeared to travel on relatively shallow paths, remain visible for several seconds, or move in ways that did not immediately match familiar meteor behaviour. The concentration of sightings near sensitive installations made the reports especially concerning to authorities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/ufos-green-fireballs-nuclear-facilities-new-mexico" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY...</span></span></span>

One of the key scientific figures drawn into the investigation was Dr Lincoln LaPaz, a mathematician and meteor specialist at the University of New Mexico. LaPaz had experience in military-related scientific work and became involved in analysing reports, collecting witness accounts, and assessing whether the objects could be explained by known atmospheric or astronomical phenomena. His correspondence from the period shows that officials were seeking explanations for reports occurring around strategically important areas.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.project1947.com/gfb/gfbchron.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: project1947.com">[Project 1947]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">project1947.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Project 1947PROJECT 1947: Green Fireball Chronology - Joel Carpenter...</span></span></span>

Several reports attracted attention because they came from trained observers rather than only members of the public. Military personnel and [scientists]({{ 'scientists/' | relative_url }}) working around New Mexico facilities contributed observations, and some incidents were examined through attempts to estimate flight paths and locations. These efforts reflected the unusual level of seriousness attached to the phenomenon during the early Cold War.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nicap.org/nmexico/newmexicosightings.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nicap.org">[Nicap]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nicap.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">newmexicosightings...</span></span></span>

The concern was partly driven by location. Los Alamos was the centre of America’s [nuclear weapons]({{ 'nuclear-weapons/' | relative_url }}) research, while nearby installations were involved in related defence activities. An unexplained object near such sites raised practical questions: could it be a natural event, an unknown aircraft, or evidence of a foreign capability? The available records show that officials investigated these possibilities but did not confirm any hostile technology or extraordinary explanation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/ufos-green-fireballs-nuclear-facilities-new-mexico" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY...</span></span></span>

## Scientific investigations and possible explanations

The most significant formal examination took place at a 1949 Los Alamos conference on aerial phenomena. Scientists and military representatives reviewed the green fireball reports and discussed possible causes. Participants included leading figures from the nuclear research community, such as Edward Teller, along with researchers familiar with atmospheric and meteor phenomena. The meeting did not reach a final explanation, but natural causes, particularly unusual meteor events, remained among the leading possibilities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://warufo.com/document/295" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: warufo.com">[WARUFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">warufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DOE-UAP-D004, Los Alamos Conference on Aerial Phenomena, 1949 — WARUFO — Declassified UAP Document ArchiveJuly 10, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 10, 2026</span></span></span>

A major challenge for investigators was that the observations did not fit neatly into a single category. Ordinary meteors could explain the brightness and colour of some reports, since atmospheric interactions can produce vivid colours. However, some witnesses believed the objects appeared to move differently from typical meteors, leading researchers to examine whether unusual atmospheric effects, high-altitude phenomena, or unknown technology could account for the observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://warufo.com/document/295" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: warufo.com">[WARUFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">warufo.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">DOE-UAP-D004, Los Alamos Conference on Aerial Phenomena, 1949 — WARUFO — Declassified UAP Document ArchiveJuly 10, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 10, 2026</span></span></span>

The investigation eventually contributed to the creation of Project Twinkle, a US Air Force effort intended to collect better scientific data on green fireballs. The project attempted to use observation equipment and photographic methods to determine whether the objects represented a natural phenomenon or something else. However, the programme produced limited results and was later discontinued, with officials leaning towards a probable natural explanation rather than a technological one.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

The investigations also illustrate the difference between uncertainty and confirmation. Scientists involved did not treat the sightings as proof of alien vehicles, but neither did they immediately dismiss every report. ## Why the mystery remained unresolved

The green fireball mystery lasted because investigators faced a basic scientific problem: the evidence was mostly eyewitness observation without enough high-quality measurements. Many reports contained useful details, but there were few photographs, no physical material, and limited opportunities to observe the events with specialised equipment. Without stronger data, researchers could narrow possibilities but could not produce a definitive identification for every sighting.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

The Cold War setting also shaped how the mystery developed. Because the sightings occurred near nuclear-related facilities, officials had reasons to take them seriously. A strange light over Los Alamos was not viewed only as a curiosity; it existed within a security environment where unknown aircraft or foreign experiments were genuine concerns. This helped turn the green fireballs into a major government investigation rather than a minor collection of unusual sky reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/ufos-green-fireballs-nuclear-facilities-new-mexico" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">When Mysterious Green Fireballs Worried the US Government &#124; HISTORY...</span></span></span>

Later UFO accounts often presented the green fireballs as one of the strongest examples of unexplained objects near nuclear sites. The historical record is more cautious. The investigations show a genuine scientific and military effort to understand unusual observations, but they do not provide evidence that the objects were extraterrestrial or that they affected nuclear operations.     return encodeURIComponent(String(value || '').replace(/\s+/g, ' ').trim()).replace(/%20/g, '+');
  }
  function buildUrl(query, reference, marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
