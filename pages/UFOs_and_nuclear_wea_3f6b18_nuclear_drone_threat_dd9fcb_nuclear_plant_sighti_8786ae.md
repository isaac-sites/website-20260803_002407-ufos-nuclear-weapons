## Introduction

Drone sightings near nuclear plants have become a practical security challenge because operators must make decisions with incomplete information. A small unmanned aircraft near a protected facility may be a hobby flight, an accidental breach, a journalist or contractor operating without approval, or a deliberate attempt to observe or interfere with the site. The difficulty is not only detecting a drone but determining what it means and how quickly authorities should respond.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/fs-drone-pwr-plant-security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDrones and Nuclear Power Plant Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionJanuary 21, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2025</span></span></span>

Civilian nuclear facilities are already protected by layered security systems, but drones introduce a different type of problem: they can appear with little warning, operate close to sensitive areas, and create uncertainty before an operator knows whether there is a genuine threat. Recent reporting from nuclear regulators, security agencies, and international organisations shows that the main challenge is managing ambiguity — separating routine drone activity from activity that could reveal hostile intent.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/fs-drone-pwr-plant-security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDrones and Nuclear Power Plant Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionJanuary 21, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2025</span></span></span>

## Reported drone activity at facilities

The number of reported drone events around nuclear facilities has increased enough for regulators to strengthen reporting requirements and monitoring arrangements. In the United States, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) states that commercial nuclear power plant operators must report drone sightings over their facilities, with reports shared with the NRC, Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI), and local law enforcement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/fs-drone-pwr-plant-security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDrones and Nuclear Power Plant Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionJanuary 21, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2025</span></span></span>

A notable example came in late 2024, when reports from US nuclear sites rose sharply during a wider period of public concern about drone activity near sensitive infrastructure. Data provided by the NRC to *The War Zone* indicated that reported drone events at nuclear facilities increased from 15 between January and 10 December 2024 to 26 by 17 December. The increase did not by itself prove a coordinated campaign; officials noted that heightened public attention and reporting behaviour could have contributed to the rise.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.twz.com/news-features/massive-uptick-in-official-drone-sightings-by-nuclear-power-plants" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: twz.com">[TWZ]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">twz.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Nuclear Power Plants Report Massive Uptick In Drone Sightings</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Power Plants Report Massive Uptick In Drone SightingsDecember 21, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 21, 2024</span></span></span>

The most serious cases have involved nuclear plants located in conflict zones, where drones are not merely [unidentified objects]({{ 'unidentified-objects/' | relative_url }}) but part of active military operations. At Ukraine’s Zaporizhzhia Nuclear Power Plant, the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) confirmed drone strikes in April 2024 and said the incidents represented a serious risk to nuclear safety and security. Although critical safety systems were not reported damaged, the agency warned that repeated military activity around nuclear facilities increased the danger of a wider accident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/Articles/Grossi-condemns-drone-strikes-at-Zaporizhzhia-site" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: world-nuclear-news.org">[World Nuclear News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">world-nuclear-news.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Nuclear News Grossi condemns drone strikes at Zaporizhzhia site</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">World Nuclear NewsGrossi condemns drone strikes at Zaporizhzhia site - World Nuclear NewsApril 8, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: April 8, 2024</span></span></span>

The distinction between ordinary sightings and wartime drone attacks is important. A commercial reactor in peacetime may face a surveillance or intrusion concern, while a plant near a battlefield faces the added risks of deliberate targeting, miscalculation, and escalation. The same basic technology creates very different security problems depending on location and circumstances.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/articles/iaea-warning-over-drones-near-nuclear-sites" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: world-nuclear-news.org">[World Nuclear News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">world-nuclear-news.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">World Nuclear News IAEA warning over drones near nuclear sites</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">World Nuclear NewsIAEA warning over drones near nuclear sites - World Nuclear NewsMay 22, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 22, 2025</span></span></span>

## Why intent is difficult to determine

A drone sighting provides only limited information. Operators may know that an aircraft entered restricted airspace, but they may not immediately know who controls it, why it is there, or what capability it has. This uncertainty creates one of the hardest parts of nuclear site security: deciding when an unknown object represents a meaningful threat.

### Detection does not equal identification

Modern counter-drone systems often combine several methods, including radar, optical cameras, acoustic sensors, and radio-frequency monitoring. Each method has limitations. Small drones can be difficult to detect against background noise, some sensors provide limited information about the operator or purpose of the flight, and weather or terrain can affect performance. Research into protected-airspace drone detection has highlighted the need for multiple sensing approaches rather than relying on a single technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.03760" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: arxiv.org">[arXiv]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">arxiv.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">arXiv Securing your Airspace: Detection of Drones Trespassing Protected Areas</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Securing your Airspace: Detection of Drones Trespassing Protected AreasNovember 5, 2021...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 5, 2021</span></span></span>

For nuclear operators, the problem is therefore not simply “spot the drone”. The more difficult questions are:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--caution" markdown="1">

* Is the drone authorised or accidental?
* Is it collecting information about the facility?
* Is it testing security responses?
* Does it carry equipment that could create a safety or security risk?
* Is it connected to a wider campaign involving other sites?

</div>

These questions often cannot be answered immediately from the sighting itself.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Plant Sightings illustration 2" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-2-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-2-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

### Surveillance concerns can exist without an attack

One reason drones attract attention around nuclear facilities is that information can have security value even without physical damage. A drone flight may reveal details about site layouts, security arrangements, construction activity, or patterns of movement. This is why unauthorised flights are treated seriously even when there is no evidence of an immediate attack.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.energy.gov/nnsa/protecting-nnsa-facilities-counter-unmanned-aircraft-systems" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: energy.gov">[The Department of Energy&#x27;s Energy.gov]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">energy.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on energy.gov.</span></span></span>

However, the existence of a security concern does not mean every incident indicates hostile intent. Authorities have repeatedly faced the challenge of avoiding both underreaction and overreaction. Treating every sighting as an attack could overwhelm response systems, while dismissing unusual activity could leave genuine threats unnoticed.



<img src="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Plant Sightings illustration 3" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-3-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/UFOs_and_nuclear_wea_3f6b18_nuclear_drone_threat_dd9fcb_nuclear_plant_sighti_8786ae-Illustration-3-light.svg" | relative_url }}" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low">

## Security responses and public concerns

Nuclear facilities have responded by improving reporting, coordination, and counter-drone planning rather than relying on a single defensive measure. In the United States, [nuclear security]({{ 'security-concerns-0719dd/' | relative_url }}) programmes already include armed security forces, physical barriers, surveillance systems, and emergency procedures designed to address a range of threats. Drone incidents add another layer to those existing protections.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/security-enhancements" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionBackgrounder on Nuclear Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDecember 5, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: December 5, 2024</span></span></span>

A major challenge is legal authority. Nuclear plant security teams do not generally have unrestricted power to disable or destroy aircraft over their sites. The NRC notes that plant security forces do not have authority to interdict or shoot down aircraft, including drones, flying over facilities. This means responses often depend on coordination with aviation authorities, law enforcement, and other government agencies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/fs-drone-pwr-plant-security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDrones and Nuclear Power Plant Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionJanuary 21, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2025</span></span></span>

The issue has also become part of wider public debates about unexplained activity around nuclear infrastructure. Historically, some reports near nuclear sites have been discussed alongside UFO or unidentified aerial phenomena claims. Modern drone incidents are different because many involve known technology, but the public reaction can still be shaped by uncertainty, limited information, and concern about nuclear risks. The practical security question is therefore not whether every unusual sighting represents something extraordinary, but whether authorities can reliably identify and manage ordinary technology being used in unusual ways.

The continuing challenge for nuclear operators is balancing caution with evidence. Strong security requires taking unauthorised drone activity seriously while maintaining a disciplined process that distinguishes confirmed threats from unexplained but harmless events. As drones become cheaper, more capable, and more common, that judgement problem is likely to remain central to nuclear site protection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nrc.gov/reading-rm/doc-collections/fact-sheets/fs-drone-pwr-plant-security" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nrc.gov">[Nuclear Regulatory Commission]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nrc.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Nuclear Regulatory CommissionDrones and Nuclear Power Plant Security &#124; Nuclear Regulatory CommissionJanuary 21, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: January 21, 2025</span></span></span>

    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
</script>
