## Introduction

Los Alamos scientists played a central but limited role in early Cold War investigations of unexplained aerial reports, especially the mysterious green fireballs observed over New Mexico from 1948 onward. Their involvement was not an effort to prove [extraterrestrial]({{ 'extraterrestrial/' | relative_url }}) explanations; rather, it reflected a scientific and national-security question: did unusual observations near nuclear facilities represent natural atmospheric events, [foreign technology]({{ 'foreign-risks/' | relative_url }}), or a previously unknown phenomenon? Scientists connected with Los Alamos brought expertise in physics, astronomy, meteorology, and measurement techniques to help evaluate reports that military officials considered potentially important.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

The most significant contribution from Los Alamos researchers came through the investigation of the green fireball sightings. These events became a notable example of scientists examining UFO reports using conventional scientific methods, while also showing the limits of Cold War-era evidence collection. The surviving record shows careful investigation and uncertainty rather than confirmation of extraordinary explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

## Researchers involved in the investigations

### The 1949 Los Alamos scientific meeting

The green fireball reports attracted attention because they occurred in a region containing major military and nuclear facilities, including Los Alamos and nearby installations involved in weapons research. Military investigators gathered reports from pilots, military personnel, and other observers before seeking scientific advice from researchers familiar with atmospheric and physical phenomena.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

In early 1949, investigators presented their findings to scientists and officials connected with Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory and other government organisations. Participants included figures associated with nuclear science and atmospheric research, such as physicist Edward Teller and meteorologist Lincoln LaPaz. The purpose was not to assume a particular cause but to determine what type of phenomenon could account for the observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

The involvement of prominent scientists helped distinguish the Los Alamos investigations from many later UFO discussions. Researchers approached the reports as an evidence problem: observations needed to be measured, compared with known physical processes, and separated from speculation. The scientific question was whether the sightings could be explained through known phenomena or whether they indicated a security concern requiring further study.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

### Working alongside military investigators

Los Alamos scientists were part of a wider network rather than an independent UFO research programme. Air Force intelligence organisations, including units investigating reports in the New Mexico region, coordinated with researchers at Los Alamos, Sandia, universities, and other federal agencies. The military interest came partly from the possibility that unusual sightings near sensitive sites might reveal foreign reconnaissance activity or new technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

This collaboration reflected the priorities of the early Cold War. Scientists at national laboratories were accustomed to analysing uncertain technical information, while military officials were focused on possible threats. UFO reports became one of several areas where scientific expertise and security concerns overlapped.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>

## Scientific methods used to examine sightings

### Comparing observations with natural explanations

Los Alamos researchers examined the physical characteristics reported by witnesses, including colour, brightness, movement, and location. The green fireballs were unusual because observers described intense green lights moving across the sky, sometimes with trajectories that did not appear to match ordinary meteor sightings. Investigators considered [meteor explanations]({{ 'meteor-explanations/' | relative_url }}) and other natural causes while also examining whether the events could have military origins.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

A key challenge was that most reports relied on human observation rather than precise instruments. Scientists needed measurements such as accurate timing, position, brightness, and photographic records to test competing explanations. The 1949 discussions led to recommendations for improved observation systems, including better photographic and scientific monitoring of future events.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

### The push for better data through Project Twinkle

The difficulty of studying the fireballs led to a more formal observation effort known as Project Twinkle. The project attempted to establish a network of observation and photographic equipment to collect more reliable data on the phenomenon. However, it faced practical limitations and did not produce the level of evidence needed to resolve the mystery completely.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

The experience demonstrated an important principle in scientific investigation: unusual observations cannot be evaluated effectively without repeatable measurements. The Los Alamos scientists’ role was therefore less about producing a final answer and more about improving the quality of evidence available to investigators.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

## Limits of the available evidence

The historical record does not show that Los Alamos scientists discovered evidence of extraterrestrial craft or technology. Later Air Force investigations, including [Project Blue Book]({{ 'project-blue-book/' | relative_url }}), concluded that UFO reports did not provide evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles or technologies beyond known science. The Air Force’s records were later transferred to the National Archives, where researchers can examine the original investigation files.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archives.gov">[National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archives.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">National Archives Project BLUE BOOK</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">National ArchivesProject BLUE BOOK - Unidentified Flying Objects &#124; National Archives...</span></span></span>

The green fireball case remained interesting because investigators could not immediately explain every observation, not because the evidence confirmed an extraordinary cause. Some characteristics appeared unusual, but incomplete measurements, inconsistent witness accounts, and limited instrumentation prevented a definitive conclusion at the time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_fireballs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Green fireballs</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Green fireballs</span></span></span>

Los Alamos scientists therefore occupy a distinctive place in UFO history. They represent a period when highly trained researchers treated unusual aerial reports as legitimate scientific questions while maintaining a cautious standard of proof. Their work shows how Cold War science approached uncertainty: collect better data, test possible explanations, and avoid conclusions that the available evidence could not support.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.osi.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2302429/project-blue-book-part-1-ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: osi.af.mil">[Office of Special Investigations]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">osi.af.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Office of Special InvestigationsProject Blue Book Part 1 (UFO Reports) &gt; Office of Special Investigations &gt; DisplayAugust 6, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 6, 2020</span></span></span>

    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
