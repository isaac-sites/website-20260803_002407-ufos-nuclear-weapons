## Introduction

The Department of Defense’s review of UAP (Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena) coordination failures found that the central problem was not simply a lack of reports, but a lack of a consistent system for collecting, comparing, and analysing them. For years, different military organisations developed their own approaches to unusual aerial observations, creating gaps between detection, reporting, intelligence analysis, and force-protection decisions. A 2023 Department of Defense Office of Inspector General (DoD OIG) evaluation concluded that the department lacked a comprehensive, coordinated approach to UAP and that different DoD components had developed varying processes for handling incidents.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>

The significance of this issue is especially clear around sensitive military environments, including areas connected with strategic aviation and nuclear forces. ## Different service reporting approaches

Before a unified process developed, UAP information moved through a patchwork of military channels. Individual services, commands, intelligence organisations, and operational units often approached unusual observations according to their existing missions rather than through a single DoD-wide framework. The result was that one organisation might treat an event primarily as a flight-safety matter, another as an intelligence concern, and another as an operational anomaly requiring local review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>

This variation created a basic governance problem: the same type of event could produce different records depending on where it occurred and who received the initial report. Military aviation units already maintain detailed procedures for aircraft incidents, airspace violations, and safety events, but UAP reports did not always fit neatly into those established categories. Without consistent guidance, analysts could struggle to determine what information should be collected, which organisations should be notified, and how incidents should be compared across the department.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/DesktopModules/ArticleCS/Print.aspx?Article=3496071&amp;ModuleId=2973&amp;PortalId=48" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the DoD&#x27;s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109)August 15, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 15, 2023</span></span></span>

The problem was particularly important because UAP cases often depend on combining information from multiple sources. A pilot observation may have greater value when matched with radar data, intelligence reporting, [satellite]({{ 'satellite/' | relative_url }}) information, or operational records. A fragmented reporting system makes those connections harder to establish because the data may remain inside separate organisational channels or be recorded in incompatible ways.

The DoD OIG review identified this fragmentation as a coordination weakness rather than a failure of individual personnel. The report examined actions by the military services, defence agencies, and military department counterintelligence organisations and found that the department needed clearer roles, responsibilities, and coordination procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/Reports/Audits-and-Evaluations/Article/3496071/evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-dodig/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous phenomena dodig</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Evaluation of the DoD&#x27;s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; Audits and Evaluat...</span></span></span>

## Inspector General findings

The most significant public assessment of the coordination problem came from the DoD Office of Inspector General’s evaluation of UAP actions. The review examined whether the department had appropriate processes to detect, report, collect, analyse, and identify UAP incidents. Its unclassified summary stated that the DoD did not have a comprehensive and coordinated approach and that components had developed differing methods for handling UAP reports.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>

The Inspector General identified several consequences of this inconsistent approach:

* **No department-wide UAP policy framework:** The DoD lacked overarching guidance that integrated UAP responsibilities into existing intelligence, counterintelligence, and force-protection systems.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>

The findings mattered because UAP reporting sits at the intersection of several existing military responsibilities. An unexplained object near a military installation may be relevant to aviation safety, airspace defence, intelligence collection, or counterintelligence. A coordination failure does not mean every unexplained event represents a serious threat, but it does mean the department may lack the information needed to make that determination efficiently.

The report led to recommendations aimed at integrating UAP procedures into existing defence systems rather than creating an entirely separate reporting culture. The Inspector General recommended that DoD establish policy covering roles, responsibilities, requirements, and coordination procedures in cooperation with AARO, the military services, and Joint Staff organisations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[DODIG]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>

## Changes toward coordinated review

The DoD response to these problems centred on creating a dedicated coordinating office. In 2022, the department established the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, expanding the role of its predecessor organisation, the Airborne Object Identification and Management Synchronization Group. AARO was designed to coordinate efforts across domains including air, space, maritime, and other environments where anomalous detections could occur.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: dodig.mil">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">dodig.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">press release evaluation of the dods actions regarding unidentified anomalous p</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Press Release: Evaluation of the DoD’s Actions Regarding Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (DODIG-2023-109) &gt; Department of War &gt; In...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

AARO’s role was not to assume that every UAP report represented unknown technology. Instead, its purpose was to improve the quality of the information available for analysis. The office emphasises collecting data, applying scientific and intelligence methods, and resolving cases where evidence allows. It also serves as a central point for coordinating reports from military and Department of Defense civilian personnel.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

AARO has established processes for official reports from military personnel, government employees, and contractors with relevant information, while military and DoD civilian personnel are directed to report through command or service channels under Joint Staff procedures.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

The shift towards central coordination has also improved the ability to build a larger picture from individual cases. UAP reports can involve sensitive sensors, classified operations, and national-security information, so a useful system must allow information sharing while protecting sources and methods.     if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
