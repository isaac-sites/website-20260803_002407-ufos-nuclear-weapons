## Introduction

The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) is the Department of Defense (DoD) organisation responsible for coordinating the military’s modern process for receiving, analysing and resolving reports of unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP). For service members and DoD civilian personnel, the reporting path is not a public sighting form but a military chain-of-command process governed by service procedures and Joint Staff guidance. AARO’s role begins when information enters the government system: it helps collect relevant data, coordinate analysis across agencies, and determine whether an observation represents a known object, a security concern, or an unresolved event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

This process matters particularly for reports connected to sensitive military environments, including areas associated with strategic operations and nuclear forces. The purpose of the system is not to assume extraordinary explanations, but to preserve operational information that may reveal airspace incursions, [foreign technology]({{ 'foreign-risks/' | relative_url }}), safety risks, sensor problems, or other defence concerns.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarDoD Announces the Establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office &gt; U.S. Department of Defense &gt; ReleaseJ...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

## Creation of a dedicated review office

### Why AARO replaced a fragmented system

Before AARO was established, UAP investigations were handled through changing structures with narrower responsibilities. The office was created in 2022 by expanding the earlier Airborne Object Identification and Management Group (AOIMSG) into a broader organisation covering anomalous objects across multiple domains, including air, space, maritime and transmedium environments. The DoD assigned AARO responsibility for synchronising efforts to detect, identify and attribute objects of interest near military installations, operating areas, training areas and other locations important to national security.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarDoD Announces the Establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office &gt; U.S. Department of Defense &gt; ReleaseJ...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

The creation of AARO represented an implementation choice: instead of treating unusual aerial observations as isolated incidents handled separately by different commands, the department created a central office able to compare reports, examine patterns and coordinate expertise. This approach is designed to reduce duplicated investigations and improve the quality of data available for assessment.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarDoD Announces the Establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office &gt; U.S. Department of Defense &gt; ReleaseJ...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

AARO’s work is organised around a data-driven assessment model. A report is treated as an unresolved observation requiring evidence collection rather than as proof of any particular explanation. The office states that many UAP cases remain unidentified because available information is insufficient to make a confident identification, not because the object has been demonstrated to possess unknown technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

## Reporting paths from military personnel

[Military reporting]({{ 'military-reporting/' | relative_url }}) begins inside existing defence structures. Current military personnel and Department of War (formerly Department of Defense) civilian personnel are required to report UAP information through their command or service channels in accordance with Joint Staff reporting guidance rather than directly through a general public reporting route.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

This distinction separates two different types of information flow:

* **Operational UAP reporting:** information generated by current military activities, such as observations by pilots, operators, aircrew, intelligence personnel, or others involved in defence operations.
* **Historical or programme-related reporting:** information from current or former government personnel, service members, or contractors who have direct knowledge of alleged government UAP-related programmes or activities. AARO created a separate reporting mechanism for this category as part of its congressionally directed historical review work.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/Next-AARO-Home-redesign/Next-Parent/Next-Submit-a-Report-dup/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Submit A Report</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO Submit A Report...</span></span></span>

A military report is most useful when it contains enough technical and contextual information to allow analysts to test possible explanations. Relevant material can include:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--phrase-stack" markdown="1">

* time and location of the observation;
* identity and role of observers;
* aircraft or platform involved;
* radar, infrared, optical or other sensor information;
* recordings and associated metadata;
* environmental conditions;
* communications and operational context.

</div>

AARO specifically highlights the value of high-quality [sensor data]({{ 'sensor-data/' | relative_url }}), timestamps, location information and other metadata because these details allow analysts to compare an observation against known aircraft, balloons, drones, satellites, atmospheric effects and other possible explanations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

For incidents near sensitive military locations, including facilities connected with strategic forces, this reporting structure provides a formal record that can be reviewed alongside existing security and intelligence processes. The significance of a report depends not only on what a witness saw but also on whether independent data can establish what occurred.

## How cases move through assessment

### From report to analysis

After information enters the system, AARO coordinates the assessment process by collecting available evidence and working with relevant defence and government organisations. The office’s mission is to detect, identify and attribute objects of interest while supporting decisions about safety and national security risks.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarDoD Announces the Establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office &gt; U.S. Department of Defense &gt; ReleaseJ...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

The assessment process generally involves several questions:

1. **What was observed?**

Analysts examine witness descriptions, sensor information and operational circumstances to establish the basic facts of the event.
2. **Can existing data identify the object?**

Many cases are resolved by matching observations with ordinary explanations such as aircraft, unmanned aerial systems, balloons, satellites or other known objects. AARO has repeatedly stated that insufficient data is a major reason some cases remain unresolved.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>
3. **Does the event indicate a safety or security issue?**

A UAP report near military operations may matter even if the final explanation is ordinary. An unidentified object entering restricted airspace, interfering with training, or creating uncertainty for operators can represent an operational concern.
4. **What additional information is required?**

Some cases remain open because analysts need better imagery, sensor records, tracking information or other evidence before reaching a conclusion.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

## What AARO changes for nuclear-related UAP reporting

Reports involving [nuclear weapons]({{ 'nuclear-weapons/' | relative_url }}) sites occupy a sensitive part of the broader military reporting environment because these locations already operate under strict security, surveillance and incident-response procedures. AARO does not replace those existing security systems; instead, it provides a specialised mechanism for analysing anomalous observations that may require broader review.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of WarDoD Announces the Establishment of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office &gt; U.S. Department of Defense &gt; ReleaseJ...</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3100053/dod-announces-the-establishment-of-the-all-domain-anomaly-resolution-office/)

The practical importance of AARO’s process is that it converts individual observations into structured records. A claim that an unusual object appeared near a military facility becomes more useful when investigators can examine the associated sensors, timelines, communications and operational circumstances. This allows analysts to distinguish between genuine unresolved events and cases where better information reveals a conventional explanation.

Within the history of UFO and nuclear-weapons discussions, this shift is significant. The modern process places emphasis on documentation, verification and cross-agency analysis rather than relying primarily on witness accounts or isolated reports. AARO’s role is therefore less about confirming or dismissing a single interpretation of UAP and more about creating a consistent government method for evaluating unusual observations near important military activities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/FAQ/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AARO FAQ...</span></span></span>

    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket];
    if (!cfg) return '#';
    var template = String(cfg.url_template || 'https://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_nkw={query}');
    var url = template.replace('{query}', encodeQuery(query || '')).replace('{query_plain}', String(query || ''));
    var joiner = url.indexOf('?') >= 0 ? '&' : '?';
    var params = [];
    if (cfg.mkrid) params.push('mkrid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.mkrid));
    if (cfg.campaign_id) params.push('campid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.campaign_id));
    params.push('mkevt=1');
    params.push('mkcid=1');
    params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
