## Introduction

The 2010 “UFOs and Nukes” National Press Club event was a turning point in the public presentation of Robert Hastings’ nuclear [UFO investigations]({{ 'ufo-case-investigation/' | relative_url }}). Held in Washington, D.C., on 27 September 2010, the conference moved the discussion from specialist UFO circles into a mainstream media setting by placing former U.S. Air Force personnel, witness affidavits, and selected declassified documents before journalists. Its impact was not that it settled the question of whether UFOs had affected [nuclear weapons]({{ 'nuclear-weapons/' | relative_url }}) systems, but that it changed the scale and tone of the debate: claims previously circulated through books, interviews, and researcher networks were presented as a coordinated public case involving military witnesses.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

## The witnesses presented at the event

The central feature of the 2010 conference was the appearance of former and retired Air Force personnel who described alleged UFO incidents connected with nuclear weapons facilities. Hastings and former Air Force Captain [Robert Salas]({{ 'robert-salas/' | relative_url }}) organised the event around first-hand accounts from witnesses who had worked in or around strategic military environments. The organisers stated that seven retired Air Force officers participated and that their accounts covered incidents involving missile fields, security operations, and unusual aerial observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

Among the most prominent witnesses was Robert Salas, a former Minuteman missile launch officer who had previously described a 1967 incident at Malmstrom Air Force Base in Montana. Salas and Hastings presented the case as part of a wider pattern in which UFO sightings allegedly occurred near [nuclear missile]({{ 'nuclear-missile/' | relative_url }}) facilities and were sometimes followed by missile-related technical problems. Other participants included former personnel associated with incidents at locations such as Malmstrom and RAF Bentwaters.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

The significance of the witness lineup was largely about credibility and framing. Hastings’ investigation had always emphasised that many reports came from military personnel who had held security clearances or operational responsibilities. Bringing these individuals to a recognised press venue allowed supporters to argue that the subject deserved serious public examination rather than dismissal as only a civilian folklore issue.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

However, the conference did not provide independent confirmation that UFOs caused nuclear system failures. The witnesses offered recollections and interpretations of events, while the question of whether [unidentified objects]({{ 'unidentified-objects/' | relative_url }}) were responsible for reported technical incidents remained contested. The difference between documenting that a report exists and proving the explanation behind it became a continuing issue in discussions after the event.

## Documents and claims discussed publicly

A major part of the conference strategy was the presentation of supporting material alongside witness testimony. Hastings and Salas distributed a press package containing witness affidavits and a selection of declassified government documents that they argued supported the historical existence of UFO reports near sensitive military locations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

The documents included material relating to earlier reports at nuclear-related facilities, including records associated with Minot Air Force Base and other Cold War-era incidents. Hastings argued that these records demonstrated a long-running pattern of official awareness of unusual aerial reports around strategic installations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

For supporters of the investigation, the combination of documents and testimony strengthened the argument that the issue was not based solely on isolated stories. The conference presented a historical narrative in which nuclear sites repeatedly appeared as locations of unusual aerial observations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://ufohastings.com/articles/the-ufos-nukes-connection-press-conference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ufohastings.com">[UFO Hastings]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ufohastings.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO Hastings UFOs &amp; Nukes</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO HastingsUFOs &amp; NukesOctober 11, 2010...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2010</span></span></span>

Critics, however, highlighted an important evidentiary distinction: a document confirming that military personnel recorded an unusual sighting does not automatically establish what the object was or whether it affected weapons systems. The 2010 event therefore increased public awareness of the reports while leaving the core interpretation disputed.

      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
